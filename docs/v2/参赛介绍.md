# 第二版参赛介绍

## 中文

《废墟里的种子：空中庭院》是一款原创3D建筑空间解谜游戏。玩家操纵一位携带种子的小园丁，穿过被水包围的旧城市。转动桥梁、乘升降台上升、打开封闭的屋顶，再为花卉、豆科和深根植物建立互补的栖息地。

礼物不是直接送到玩家手上的世界，而是一颗留给废弃旧世界的种子。只有有人愿意穿过废墟，重新连接水、光和生命，它才真正成立。

作品借鉴建筑解谜中的构图留白、机关变化与克制叙事，使用原创角色、模型和关卡。三维视角可以环绕观察；桥梁、阶梯和升降台有真实的空间关系。生态主题通过改变建筑与生命反馈表达，十二处短记忆补充旧城市的失败原因。

主题：A Gift for the Abandoned World。完整主线离线可玩，包含三个庭院、两个结局、自动保存和种子纪念卡。

## English

Seed in the Ruins: The Floating Gardens is an original 3D architectural puzzle game. A small gardener carries a seed through the remains of a city surrounded by water. Rotate bridges, ride a lift, open a sealed roof and make room for complementary plant life.

The gift is a seed left for an abandoned world. It becomes a gift only when someone explores the ruins and reconnects water, light and living habitats.

The game uses restrained storytelling and architectural composition, with original characters, geometry and level layouts. Its camera can orbit the scene, and bridges, stairs and lifts occupy real three-dimensional space. Twelve optional memories reveal how a city pursuing efficiency lost its capacity to recover.

A Gift for the Abandoned World. Three playable gardens, two endings, automatic saves and an exportable seed card. The complete experience works offline.
