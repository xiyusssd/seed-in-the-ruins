# 废墟里的种子 / Seed in the Ruins

你从一座失去颜色的废墟中醒来。留下的种子没有收件人，十二条城市线索却记录着水、日光和彼此补足的生命。你用第一人称视角穿过桥梁和阶梯，转动机关、升起日光台，让一份礼物走到可以生长的地方。

每发现一条新的线索，整个世界就找回一点颜色。开始只有黑白的水、天空和石塔；完整找到十二条线索后，它们重新拥有自己的颜色。低沉风声和远处的金属回响，也随着生命的回归增加新的声部。

作品主题：A Gift for the Abandoned World。礼物是一颗种子，也是一种让废弃世界重新生长的可能。三个原创3D庭院、实际空间机关、十二条线索和两个结局构成当前可玩的第一人称Demo。场景和音频由项目生成，无运行时AI依赖。附1.3桌面包，以及明确标注为1.1历史素材的自动化游览录屏和视觉素材板；未上传平台。

## English

Wake in a ruin that has lost its colour. Explore three original 3D gardens in first person, turn physical bridges, ride a lift, and open a roof to return sunlight to plants. Twelve unique traces of the abandoned city gradually restore colour to the entire world. Sparse wind, low drones, and distant metallic echoes gain a warmer layer as colour returns. The final gift is a seed—and a choice between preserving the old city's container and letting new life grow beyond it.

The playable 1.3 demo includes two endings, independent saves, original generated geometry and audio. Seedling inventory, reversible controls and archived notes reduce repetitive travel and support environmental reasoning. The included screenshots and unedited automated walkthrough are historical 1.1 media. It runs offline without a live AI service.
